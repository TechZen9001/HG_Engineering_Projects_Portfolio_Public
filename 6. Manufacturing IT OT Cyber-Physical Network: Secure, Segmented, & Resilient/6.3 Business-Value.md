# Project 6. Manufacturing IT / OT Cyber-Physical Network: Secure, Segmented, & Resilient

## 6.3 Business Value

Project 6 demonstrates how secure IT/OT segmentation, access control, redundant connectivity, and centralized monitoring can reduce cybersecurity exposure, improve manufacturing reliability, and support business continuity.
